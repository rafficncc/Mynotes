# FamilyFin Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

FamilyFin ("the app") is a family expense tracker. This policy explains what happens to your information.

## Your data stays on your phone
Everything you enter (accounts, transactions, bills, documents, photos of bills, notes) is stored **only on your device**,
encrypted with a key kept in your phone's secure storage. We (the developer) do not run servers that receive your data, and we
cannot see it.

## Google sign-in and Google Drive backup (optional)
If you choose to connect a Google account, the app reads your **name and email address** to show which account is connected, and saves
a backup file to a **private app folder in your own Google Drive**. The app can only see its own backup file there, not your other files.
The backup goes directly from your phone to Google; it does not pass through us. You can disconnect at any time in Settings.
Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Purchases
FamilyFin Premium is bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store. They handle payment; we never see your card or payment details.

## Permissions
- **Camera / photos**: to scan a bill or attach a bill photo, only when you choose to.
- **Microphone / speech recognition**: for voice entry, only while you are speaking an expense.
- **Notifications**: for the bill, EMI and document reminders you set.
- **Fingerprint / face**: to unlock the app; handled by your phone, the app never receives your biometric data.

## Sharing
We do not sell or share your data, and the app has no ads or analytics trackers.

## Deleting your data
Use **Settings → Erase all data** (protected by your app PIN), or uninstall the app. To remove a Drive backup, disconnect the app
in your Google account settings (Google account → Security → Third-party apps) or delete the FamilyFin app data from Google Drive settings (Manage apps).

## Children
FamilyFin is meant for adults managing family money and is not directed at children under 13.
